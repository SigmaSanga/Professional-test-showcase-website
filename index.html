The arranged day

You decide to play. We handle every step from there.

Tell the club once, by message or a single tap, and the rest of the morning sorts itself out. Our staff take care of everything, so you never deal with a system.

Membership

One hundred and eighty cards. Never one more.

Membership is by introduction from two members, then a round with the Captain. The committee sits twice a year. We keep the numbers small so there is always a tee and never a queue.

  • i.A personal secretary who arranges your golf, cars and guests
  • ii.A tee held for you every weekend from dawn until nine
  • iii.Six dormy rooms for guests, and reciprocal play at eleven clubs
  • iv.No bills and no tills: one statement each quarter
